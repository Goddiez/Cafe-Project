OUR STORY

Simple things,
done thoughtfully.

Mysa began as an idea for a neighbourhood café where the pace feels a little softer. The name is inspired by the feeling of being comfortable, content and completely at ease.

Our approach is uncomplicated: carefully sourced coffee, food made fresh, seasonal ingredients where possible, and a room that welcomes both five-minute espresso stops and three-hour conversations.

“We wanted to make the kind of place we’d keep coming back to.”
